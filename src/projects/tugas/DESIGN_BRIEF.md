# Tugas — Design Brief

## What is it?
Tugas is a simple, mobile-first task manager. It's the example project that ships with Bengkel Design Lab, demonstrating every framework feature in a familiar context.

## Who is it for?
- **Primary:** Junior developers and students who want a lightweight task manager that's faster than sticky notes
- **Secondary:** Anyone evaluating Bengkel Design Lab as a framework — Tugas is the reference implementation

## Business Goals
1. Demonstrate all framework features: multi-screen routing, design tokens, persona feedback, decision logging, scenario stepper
2. Be simple enough to understand in 5 minutes but complete enough to serve as a template
3. Feel like a real app, not a demo — realistic mock data, thoughtful UI, Indonesian localization

## Core Problem
People use sticky notes, Notion, or nothing at all to track tasks. Tugas is the "just enough" option — open, write, done. No sign-up, no complexity.

## Vision
A task manager that respects your time. Open it, see what matters, close it.
